# Image provenance

Every image in this directory is **AI-generated**. None is a photograph, none
depicts a real person, place or event, and none is licensed from a third party —
so there is no attribution to render and nothing here needs a credit line on the
site.

This file exists because that is not visible from the files themselves. Without
it, the next person to look at a directory of photorealistic JPEGs has to work
out whether the project is out of compliance with a stock licence, and the safe
assumption in that situation is the wrong one.

## What follows from it

- **They are decorative.** Alt text describes what is depicted so a screen
  reader is not left with a gap, but no image carries information the surrounding
  text does not already give.
- **Nothing presents them as evidence.** The "How it lands" section on the
  landing page pairs three of them with role labels — graduate student, product
  team, daily reader — and the copy is written about what the app does, in the
  present tense, rather than about what it did for someone. A generated portrait
  beside a past-tense claim reads as a customer testimonial, and there are no
  customers being quoted. Worth re-reading that section if the copy is ever
  reworked.
- **Real testimonials, if there ever are any, need real attribution** and should
  not reuse these images.
