NeuroLens

Reading should feel this easy on every page you open.

Bring your settings across

Open NeuroLens in this browser and your typeface, spacing and colours come across on their own. The extension has no sign-in of its own.

What to change

Browsers keep their own pages and web stores off limits to every extension, so there is nothing to change here.

Your settings stay in this browser, and nothing about the pages you visit is sent anywhere.
